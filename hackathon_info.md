# ML Gradient Wars 2026

A 48-hour ML battle. ₹5 lakh in prizes, and every member of the top 3 teams is referred to The Data Foundation for a 3-month internship.

Source: https://hack4hack.com/hackathons/ml-gradient-wars-2026-5tns

ML Gradient Wars 2026 is a **48-hour, paid-entry machine-learning battle**. One round, one dataset, one leaderboard. **The problem statement and dataset are out now: UPI Fraud Rings.** The leaderboard opens on **13 Nov, 10:00 AM IST**, and the round closes on **15 Nov at 10:00 AM IST**.

**₹5 lakh in prizes**, and **every member of the top 3 teams is referred to The Data Foundation for a 3-month internship**.

## How it works
1. **Register** as a team of 1 to 4 members and pay the **₹1,999 entry fee per team**, by **13 Nov, 10:00 AM IST**, when the round starts.
2. **The problem statement and dataset are out now** under Resources in your team workspace: data, starter notebook and full problem statement in one zip. **The leaderboard opens on 13 Nov, 10:00 AM IST.**
3. **The 48-hour live round: 13 Nov, 10:00 AM to 15 Nov, 10:00 AM IST.** Upload predictions from the **Leaderboard** tab, up to **10 times a day**. The leaderboard updates every 15 minutes.
4. **Upload prediction CSVs on the Leaderboard tab.** Once your team is on the leaderboard (at least one scored upload), **submit your code** (GitHub repository or notebook link) and a short note on your approach from the Submission tab, before the round closes. Teams that are not on the leaderboard cannot submit.
5. **Final ranking and code verification on 15 Nov**, on the private part of the test set. **Results at 5:00 PM IST.**

- The **final ranking** uses the hidden private part, based on each team's best public upload. Models that generalise win; overfitting the leaderboard does not.

## Rules
- Take part in a team of 1 to 4 members. Each person can be in only one team, with one account.
- Each team pays a ₹1,999 entry fee (per team, not per person) when registering. A team is registered only once the fee is paid.
- There is one 48-hour round: 13 Nov, 10:00 AM to 15 Nov, 10:00 AM IST. The problem statement and dataset are released at the start.
- Train only on the dataset provided. Open-source libraries and publicly available pretrained weights are allowed; outside labelled data for the same task and hand-labelling the test set are not.

- Internship referrals go to every member of the top 3 teams in the final ranking. The internship itself is offered at The Data Foundation’s discretion after their interview.
- The organizers' decisions on verification and results are final.
